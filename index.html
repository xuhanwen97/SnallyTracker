              How scoring works

              Every pour adds alcohol units (AU) to your hoard: ounces times ABV. A standard 4 oz pour of a 6.5% beer is 26 AU.

              Pour AUAU = oz × ABV%
              Total AUTotal AU = Σ (oz × ABV%) over every counted pour
              Average ABVAvg ABV = Total AU ÷ Total oz (volume-weighted)
